INVIGIL — QUESTION PAPER TEMPLATE
=================================

Fill this in, save it as PDF, Word (.docx) or plain text, and upload it under
Import. Delete these instructions before uploading — or leave them, they are
ignored.

RULES
- "Section:" applies to every question below it, until the next Section line.
- Number questions 1. or 1) or Q1. or Q.1
- Options may be (A) or A) or A. and may be lower case.
- "Answer:" may be a letter, a number, or the option's own text.
- "Explanation:", "Topic:" and "Difficulty:" are optional.
- Leave a blank line between questions.
- The blank rows at the end are for you to fill in. Anything left empty is
  reported and skipped, never imported half-finished.

--------------------------------- EXAMPLE ---------------------------------

Section: Quantitative Aptitude

1. A train 180 m long crosses a pole in 12 seconds. What is its speed?
(A) 54 km/h
(B) 60 km/h
(C) 45 km/h
(D) 72 km/h
Answer: A
Explanation: Speed = 180 / 12 = 15 m/s = 15 x 18/5 = 54 km/h.
Topic: Speed and distance
Difficulty: Easy

2. Simplify: 25% of 480 + 15% of 200
(A) 140
(B) 150
(C) 130
(D) 160
Answer: B
Explanation: 120 + 30 = 150.
Topic: Percentages
Difficulty: Easy

Section: General Awareness

3. Who wrote the national anthem of India?
(A) Bankim Chandra Chatterjee
(B) Rabindranath Tagore
(C) Sarojini Naidu
(D) Muhammad Iqbal
Answer: B
Explanation: Rabindranath Tagore wrote Jana Gana Mana in 1911.
Topic: Modern history
